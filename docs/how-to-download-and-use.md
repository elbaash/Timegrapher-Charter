# How to Download & Use ChronoGrapher

ChronoGrapher is a **free app for watchmakers**. You photograph your Weishi timegrapher's display and the app reads the numbers for you — Rate, Amplitude, Beat Error and Lift Angle — then keeps a dated history per watch so you can prove the improvement across regulation passes.

**Everything runs on your phone.** No account, no internet needed after install, no photos ever leave your device, nothing to pay.

---

## 1. Download & Install (one time)

ChronoGrapher installs like a normal app, straight from your phone's browser. You need internet **only for this first step**.

**Open this address on your phone:** <https://elbaash.github.io/Timegrapher-Charter/>

> **Tip:** email or message yourself the link and tap it on your phone — typing a web address on a small keyboard is fiddly.

### Android (Chrome)

1. Open the link above in Chrome.
2. When the **"Add to Home Screen"** banner appears, tap **Install / Add**.
   (No banner? Tap the **⋮** menu in the top-right → **Add to Home Screen**.)
3. The ChronoGrapher icon appears on your home screen like any other app.

### iPhone (Safari — must be Safari, not Chrome)

1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the upward arrow, bottom-centre).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. The ChronoGrapher icon appears on your home screen like any other app.

> **First launch:** open the app once while online. The first visit downloads the text-recognition engine (~28 MB). From then on the app works **completely offline** — you can even switch on airplane mode.

## 2. Your First Session — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Set up your machine

1. Seat the watch securely in the Weishi microphone clip (avoid clamping on the crown).
2. Set the correct **lift angle** for the movement (most modern calibers: 52°; e.g. ETA 2824-2 / Sellita SW200 = 50°, Seiko 4R = 52°).
3. Let the reading stabilize for 30–60 seconds, and press **Start/Stop** on the machine to lock the numbers on screen.

### Step 2 — Photograph the display

1. Open ChronoGrapher → **New** tab → **Batch photos**.
2. Work through the six positions in the order printed on the machine:
   **Dial Down → Crown Up → Crown Down → Crown Left → Crown Right → Dial Up**
3. Take one photo per position, straight-on, with the numbers clearly visible. You can take the photos in the app (camera button) or pick them from your gallery afterwards.
4. The app labels each photo with its position automatically in that order.

> **Tip:** on wide or angled shots, tap the **crop** icon on a photo and frame just the timegrapher screen — it makes the reading far more reliable.

### Step 3 — Review the numbers

The app jumps to the **Review** tab and shows what it read from each photo.

- Check each reading against the machine's screen.
- **Amber fields** mean the app couldn't read that number — type it in by hand.
- Correct anything wrong (the most common miss is the +/− sign on the Rate), adjust positions if needed.
- Tap **Confirm** to add the readings to your workspace.

Photo not cooperating at all? Use **New → Manual** to type a reading in by hand.

### Step 4 — Save to a watch

Enter the watch's name (and reference, if you like) and tap **Save to Watch**. This stores the readings as a **dated table** under that watch. If the watch doesn't exist yet it's created for you.

### Step 5 — Regulate and repeat

Adjust the watch, then run Steps 2–4 again. Each save adds a new dated table, so your history builds up pass by pass.

### Step 6 — Compare progress & share

Open the **Watches** tab and select your watch:

- **Timeline** — scroll every dated table you've saved (evidence of the work done).
- **Compare** — a grid of position × attempt for each metric, so you can watch the numbers converge.
- **Share** — send a clean PDF report of any dated table (or the full comparison) to a customer or colleague via your phone's share sheet.

## 3. Back Up Your Data (important!)

All data lives **only on your phone** — there is no cloud. Phones get lost, and iPhones in particular can clear app data when storage runs low.

- **Watches** tab → **Export backup** — saves a small JSON file with your entire history. Keep it somewhere safe (email it to yourself, save to Drive, etc.).
- **Import backup** on the same tab restores everything — on this phone or any other device. Merging is safe: importing the same file twice won't duplicate your data.

> **Make it a habit:** export a backup every few days of bench work.

## 5. Quick Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| App won't install / no "Add to Home Screen" | Make sure you're using Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), and that you're online |
| Blank fields (amber) after a scan | Tap crop and frame just the screen, retake the photo closer and straight-on, or fill the field in by hand |
| Rate sign (+/−) looks wrong | Correct it in Review before saving — this is the most common OCR slip |
| App is slow the very first time | Normal — it's loading the ~28 MB text-recognition engine; instant after that |
| Lost my data after a phone update | Restore from your exported backup file (Watches → Import backup). This is why regular exports matter |

---

## 6. Good to Know

- **Targets for a healthy result:** Beat Error under 0.5 ms, Amplitude 270°–310° at full wind; chronometer-grade rate is −4/+6 s/d.
- **Privacy:** photos are processed entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere, ever.
- **Updating:** when a new version is published, just open the app once while online — it updates itself automatically.
- **Free forever:** no accounts, no subscriptions, no per-scan cost.
